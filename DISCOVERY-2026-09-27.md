# Discovery evidence: resort activity and accommodation businesses

Date checked: 2026-09-27
Status: initial public-source scan; findings are leads, not a complete market count or customer validation.

## Work completed

- Compared resort-published winter activity information and booking routes at Big White, SilverStar and Apex.
- Traced public-facing activity-provider leads around the resorts. The current working map records four initial providers and keeps nearby, transport and guiding candidates separate. It does not establish a complete count or verified independent ownership.
- Expanded the scan to accommodation: who manages on-mountain rentals, where guests are directed to book, and whether separate property managers serve owners.

## Accommodation findings

- **Big White:** Big White Central Reservations describes itself as the resort’s official accommodation provider and advertises property management and direct booking. Separate businesses also advertise Big White rental management, including Luxury Mountain Vacation Rentals, Duval Property Management, Lifty Life, Sierra Vacation Rentals and others. Luxury Mountain Vacation Rentals describes itself as locally owned and says Allura Direct processes bookings and payments made through its site.
- **SilverStar:** The public rental-management directory lists resort management for several buildings and owner homes, as well as separate operators including Vance Creek Hotel & Conference Centre and Silver Star Stays. Silver Star Stays describes itself as locally owned and offering hands-on rental management.
- **Apex:** The resort accommodation page directs guests to Stay At Apex and Apex Mountain Lodge. Stay At Apex advertises its own rental inventory and property-management service.

This suggests a mixed accommodation model: resort-affiliated services, distinct rental managers, lodging businesses and individual owners may all participate. The scan does not verify legal ownership, current inventory, contracts, booking merchant, or who can approve changes across every listing channel.

## Customer-fit hypothesis

Accommodation managers may be a useful adjacent segment to compare with activity operators. A manager representing multiple properties may maintain guest-facing descriptions, amenities, access details, policies and booking information across several channels. That could make the manager a practical buyer for a small AI visibility or information-accuracy experiment.

This is a hypothesis only. No accommodation business has been contacted, no customer demand or willingness to pay has been validated, and the project’s current starting audience remains independent tour and activity operators. The next useful step is to compare one small rental manager at each resort against the activity-operator profile and check whether each controls enough public information for a measurable test.
